What this is: a shortened version of the Implicit Association Test. Sorting is faster when two concepts that feel linked share a button. The gap between rounds is the association.

What this is not: a valid measure of you. The real test uses about 180 trials; this uses 32 scored ones. Treat the room's pattern as the interesting part, not your own score.
